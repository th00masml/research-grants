# NSF Project Summary - Large Language Models Research

## Overview
This project advances the fundamental understanding and reliable application of Large Language Models (LLMs) through research in mechanistic interpretability, alignment, efficiency, and domain integration. LLMs represent transformative technology with enormous potential for scientific discovery, but critical gaps remain in understanding their reasoning, controlling their behavior, and deploying them reliably.

## Intellectual Merit

### Research Significance
This work addresses fundamental questions about how LLMs encode, retrieve, and reason over knowledge. Current approaches to interpreting and improving LLMs are largely empirical; this project develops principled mechanistic approaches that enable:

1. **Understanding**: Causal analysis of how LLMs transform inputs to outputs
2. **Improvement**: Direct manipulation of internal mechanisms to enhance capabilities
3. **Reliability**: Verification methods ensuring consistent, trustworthy outputs
4. **Scaling**: Mechanisms for efficient scaling without proportional resource increases

### Innovation
Our approach innovates through:
- **Mechanistic interpretability**: Move beyond black-box analysis to causal circuit analysis
- **Integrated alignment**: Combine interpretability insights with alignment techniques
- **Efficient specialization**: Enable domain expertise without full retraining
- **Multimodal reasoning**: Extend to vision, embodied understanding, and structured knowledge

### Research Plan
Five integrated research thrusts:

**Thrust 1: Mechanistic Interpretability (Year 1)**
- Develop tools to identify interpretable components within LLMs
- Create causal graphs connecting internal components to outputs
- Validate interpretations through component ablation and modification
- Deliverable: Interpretability toolkit and analysis framework

**Thrust 2: Alignment & Consistency (Year 1-2)**
- Create training methods enforcing logical consistency
- Develop verification systems for hallucination detection
- Implement uncertainty quantification mechanisms
- Deliverable: Alignment techniques and evaluation benchmarks

**Thrust 3: Efficient Scaling (Year 2)**
- Design knowledge distillation from large to efficient models
- Implement sparse and adaptive computation mechanisms
- Create few-shot specialization without catastrophic forgetting
- Deliverable: Efficient model variants and adaptation methods

**Thrust 4: Domain Integration (Year 2-3)**
- Combine LLMs with structured domain knowledge
- Implement retrieval-augmented generation for specialized domains
- Evaluate on scientific and professional benchmarks
- Deliverable: Domain-specialized models and evaluation suites

**Thrust 5: Multimodal Understanding (Year 3)**
- Integrate visual, textual, and embodied representations
- Develop reasoning over multimodal scientific data
- Create connections to robotic and physical reasoning
- Deliverable: Multimodal models and benchmarks
