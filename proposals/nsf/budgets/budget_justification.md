## J. JUSTIFICATION FOR KEY DECISIONS

### Personnel Mix

**Rationale for 2 PhD Students**: 
- Distribute workload across 5 Aims
- PhD graduates will advance interpretable AI workforce
- Multiple perspectives strengthen research rigor

**Why Postdoc Over Additional PhD?**
- Research momentum requires experienced researcher
- Postdoc can manage collaborations and publications
- Faster progress on technical milestones

### Equipment Investment

**Why Not Just Cloud?**
- On-campus GPU control and reliability
- Lower long-term cost ($141K over 5 years vs. $300K+ cloud)
- Shared resource benefits broader institution
- Supports teaching and outreach

### Publication Costs

**Open Access Justification**:
- NSF requirement for publicly-funded research
- Broader dissemination and impact
- Aligns with FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interpretable, Reusable) principles
