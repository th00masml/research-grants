# Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback

**Authors:** Long Ouyang, Jeff Wu, Xu Jiang, et al. (10 total)
**Published:** 2022-03-04
**arXiv:** [2203.02155](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02155)

## Abstract

Making language models bigger does not inherently make them better at following a user's intent. For example, larger models can be more sycophantic and exhibit undesired behaviors. In order to make language models more helpful, harmless, and honest, we fine-tune language models with human feedback, using reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF).
